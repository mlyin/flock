Make room for what comes next. Flock helps you organize the clothes you want to sell, prepare listing drafts, and keep track of your resale inventory.

START WITH YOUR PHOTOS
Add photos of garments and labels. Keep the details that matter together, including brand, size, condition, and your own notes.

PREPARE YOUR LISTINGS
Review item details and prepare listing copy from one place. Keep your descriptions and asking prices organized as you get ready to sell.

KNOW YOUR NUMBERS
Track what you paid, what you are asking, and the lowest price you would accept. Review estimated marketplace fees when planning your prices.

KEEP YOUR INVENTORY TOGETHER
Return to your items, drafts, and recorded sales from your Flock account, with access on the web at sellonflock.com.

Flock is an independent resale tool. Marketplace accounts and their terms apply separately. Preparing a draft in Flock does not publish it to a marketplace, and fee estimates do not guarantee sale proceeds.

A Flock account and internet connection are required.
