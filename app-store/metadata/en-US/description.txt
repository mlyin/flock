Make room for what comes next. Flock helps you organize the clothes you want to sell, prepare listing drafts, and keep track of your resale inventory.

START WITH A PHOTO
Photograph a garment or choose a photo from your library. Keep its brand, size, condition, category, and your own notes together.

PREPARE YOUR LISTINGS
Turn your item details into a draft you can share using the iPhone share sheet. Preparing or sharing a draft does not publish a marketplace listing.

KNOW YOUR NUMBERS
Track what you paid, your asking price, and the lowest price you would accept. Your private cost and minimum price stay out of the shared draft.

KEEP YOUR INVENTORY TOGETHER
Search your closet, edit garment details, and access your inventory through your Flock account.

TRY A SAMPLE CLOSET
Explore sample garments before signing in. Sample changes stay in the current session and do not change your saved inventory.

A Flock account and internet connection are required to save your own inventory. Flock is an independent resale tool.
